Our Story

The line every great build starts from.

In carpentry, the centerline is where every accurate measurement begins. In 2020, Alfred Tudela founded Centerline Construction Company, LLC on the same idea — that every project, and every relationship, should start from a true center: faith, integrity, and old-fashioned craftsmanship.

With more than 18 years of residential and commercial construction experience behind it, Centerline is carried by a crew of skilled carpenters and craftsmen who work closely with every client to bring their vision to life — on time, on budget, and with the precision and attention to detail your home deserves. We're a Christ-centered, family-based company, and we build like it: honestly, carefully, and with a handshake that still means something.

Meet the owner

Alfred Tudela, founder & CEO

Alfred didn't build Centerline from behind a desk. With over 18 years in residential and commercial construction, he still shows up on your job site, meets you on day one, and swings a hammer alongside his crew. Clients call him honest, hard-working, and enthusiastic — he calls it simply doing the job right.

And he'd be the first to tell you Centerline isn't a one-man show. The credit belongs to the whole crew — the carpenters, tile setters, and finish craftsmen who show up early, keep the job site clean, and treat every home like their own. Hire Centerline and you get all of them.

Family is the heart of everything Alfred builds. A devoted husband and father, he founded Centerline as a family company — one where the values taught at home show up on every job site: keep your word, respect people's homes, and do work you'd be proud to put your name on.

Faith comes first. When Alfred isn't building for clients, he's building into the next generation as the Youth Director at Rising Hills Church in Canton, Georgia — mentoring students and serving the same North Georgia community his company calls home.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns Centerline Construction Company?

Centerline Construction Company, LLC was founded and is owned by Alfred Tudela, a hands-on CEO with more than 18 years of experience in residential and commercial construction. He personally meets with clients and works alongside his team on projects across North Georgia.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Holly Springs, Georgia and serve Canton, Woodstock, Cherokee County, and communities across North Georgia and the greater Atlanta area.

What services do you offer?

Bathroom remodels, basement finishing, kitchen remodels, home additions, custom decks and railing, siding, wood rot repair, cabinetry, exterior repairs, and commercial renovations.

What makes Centerline different?

We're a Christ-centered, family-owned company built on faith, integrity, and craftsmanship. Clients consistently tell us we're honest, hard-working, and always show up when promised — and that the owner is personally involved in every project.

How do I get a free consultation?

Call or text (678) 372-1274. We're available Monday–Saturday 7:00am–6:00pm and Sunday 2:00pm–6:00pm.
