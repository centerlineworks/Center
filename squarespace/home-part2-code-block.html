Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Holly Springs, Georgia and serve Canton, Woodstock, Ball Ground, Acworth, Marietta, Cherokee County and communities throughout North Georgia and the greater Atlanta area.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — estimates are free. Call or text (678) 372-1274 or request one online. You'll get a clear quote and timeline up front, and the price you're quoted is the price you pay.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Bathroom remodels, basement finishing, custom cabanas and pavilions, home additions, decks and railing, siding, wood rot repair, cabinetry, exterior repairs and commercial renovations — from focused repairs to full-scale remodels.

Who owns Centerline Construction?

Alfred Tudela founded and owns Centerline Construction Company, LLC. He has 18+ years of construction experience and is personally involved in projects — meeting clients and working alongside his crew.

What are your hours?

Monday–Saturday 7:00am–6:00pm and Sunday 2:00pm–6:00pm.
